Who We Are

Fostering People's Education, Empowerment and Enterprise, Inc. (FosPEEE)

FosPEEE is a non-profit, non-stock, non-government organization that aims to promote community empowerment for environmental stewardship, restoration, and conservation of biodiversity, and sustainable development in key conservation sites in the Philippines.

Our Vision

A recognized leader in building environment-friendly social enterprises among sustainable, empowered, and resilient communities living in biodiversity-rich ecosystems

Our Mission

An organization of committed environmental advocates fostering people’s social enterprise, education, and empowerment.

Our Identity

FosPEEE Logo Transition

FosPEEE transitioned its acronym from FPE3 to FosPEEE, while retaining its full organizational name. This rebranding includes a new logo that clearly represents Fostering People’s Education, Empowerment, and Enterprise Inc.

2017 – 2022

2022 – 2025

2025 to Present

The new logo design highlights the key thrusts that FosPEEE promotes through its work. These key thrusts are represented by three (3) distinct elements – each with its own color and shape:

  • The 1st element is the brown, tree trunk-shaped human figure that represents the Community;
  • The 2nd element consists of (3) three leaves in various colors, representing the three thematic areas of FosPEEE, the blue leaf stands for “Education”, the red leaf stands for “Empowerment”, and the golden yellow leaf stands for “Enterprise”; and
  • The 3rd element is a hollowed green circle enclosing the human figure and the leaves, symbolizing Mother Earth / the Environment.

Our Purpose

To promote community empowerment for environmental stewardship, restoration and conservation of biodiversity, and sustainable development.

To foster the education, empowerment, and social enterprise of people and communities that encourage biodiversity conservation and sustainable development.

To promote, develop, implement, engage in, and/or support biodiversity-friendly social enterprises.

To collaborate and partner with other civil society organizations, local government units, national government agencies, academe, and other stakeholders to promote environmental stewardship, restoration and conservation of biodiversity, and sustainable development.

To generate resources through fundraising to pursue environmental stewardship, restoration and conservation of biodiversity, and sustainable development.
