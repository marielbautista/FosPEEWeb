Our Network of Partners

Collaboration is the foundation of our mission. We work with a diverse network of organizations, from government agencies to local communities, to scale our conservation and empowerment efforts.

BANTAY IPO PROJECT PARTNERS

In line with the MWSS and the 2 Concessionaires (MWSI & MWCI) 25 years Integrated Watershed Management Roadmap for Angat-Ipo-La Mesa (IWMRAIL 2023-2047), FosPEEE is awarded as Service Provider for the implementation of the 2-Year Management of Bantay Gubat and Strengthening of the Ipo Watershed Protection System, also known as the Bantay Ipo Project.

PARTNER PEOPLE’S ORGANIZATION

In 2010, FosPEEE was engaged by the Manila Water Company Inc. (MWCI) as the Service Provider for the Project Community Preparation for Tayabasan Sub-watershed and Rehabilitation and Restoration Program for the Boso-boso Sub-watershed. This initiative led to the organization of the 11 People’s Organization (PO) and the implementation of rainforestation activities covering approximately 500 hectares within the Upper Marikina River Basin Protected Landscape.

Since then, these POs have remained key partners of FosPEEE in the implementation of its various projects within the UMRBPL.
