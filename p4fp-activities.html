Partners for Project Activities

Developing Legacy Forest through Rainforestation and Partnership Building.

1. Tree-planting Activities

Partners for Forest is a tree-planting initiative designed to engage the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs of private sector partners. Through this initiative, FosPEEE secures sponsorship and support from private schools and companies to implement tree-growing and forest restoration activities.
