Partners for Forest Project (P4FP)

Developing Legacy Forest through Rainforestation and Partnership Building.

About The Project

Partners for Forest is an initiative that empowers communities to lead and manage conservation efforts aimed at restoring forest lands within Protected Areas in the Philippines, particularly the Upper Marikina River Basin Protected Landscape (UMRBPL).

The project targets the planting of more than 6,000 native tree species annually, covering at least ten (10) hectares of forest through the active engagement and support of various stakeholders, especially the private sector.

Specifically, the initiative seeks to:

  • Restore and enhance the vegetative cover within the Upper Marikina River Basin Protected Landscape (UMRBPL);
  • Promote the use of Rainforestation Technology by utilizing native tree species in reforestation efforts within UMRBPL; and
  • Strengthen community participation in conservation initiatives and sustainable livelihood opportunities through the implementation and management of the Partners for Forest and Rainfo-Tourism Program.
